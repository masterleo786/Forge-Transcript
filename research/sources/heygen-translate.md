## FAQs About Our AI Video Translator

### What is a video translator?

A video translator is a tool that converts audio, voiceover, or subtitles in a video into another language, helping content reach a wider audience. This process allows content creators, businesses, and organizations to expand their reach globally by overcoming language barriers.

### Can I use AI to translate a video?
